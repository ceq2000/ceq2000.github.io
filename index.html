About me

I'm Carlos, a bilingual consultant who has spent more than two decades shipping web platforms, CMS rollouts and CRM projects for banks, publishers and global brands. Today I focus on AI process automation: finding the repetitive, copy-and-paste work inside a business and turning it into reliable workflows, whether that's routing a new lead, processing an invoice or answering a routine support request.

I work discovery-first. Before anything is built, we measure where the hours go and agree on what is worth automating. You always talk to the person doing the work.
